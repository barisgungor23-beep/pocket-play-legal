Who we are

Pocket Play is provided by Barış Güngör — Independent Developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains information handled by the iOS app and these legal pages. For privacy questions or requests, contact gungorb860@gmail.com.

Information on your device

Pocket Play stores your game records, favorites, recently opened games, sound and haptic preferences, music settings, language choice, onboarding completion, active gameplay and trial time, and review-prompt history locally. It also stores downloaded announcements and their display history locally. These records support app features and prevent repeated prompts. The app does not upload these gameplay records or preferences to a developer-operated server. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data, depending on your settings.

Purchases and identifiers

Apple processes App Store payments. Pocket Play uses RevenueCat to retrieve products, validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine Premium access. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information, such as product and transaction identifiers, receipt or signed transaction information, purchase and expiry dates, and entitlement status. Its dashboard also provides purchase history and subscription reporting.

The app does not require a Pocket Play account or ask you to provide your name or email to play. RevenueCat generates a random app user ID and caches it on your device. This is a pseudonymous identifier, not a guarantee that purchase information is unidentifiable. The SDK also sends technical information such as the device’s identifier for vendor, device model, OS and app versions, preferred locales, storefront, and purchase currency. Network services receive your IP address when a connection is made. Pocket Play does not receive your full payment card details or Apple Account password.

Announcements and legal pages

The app requests a public announcement feed hosted on GitHub. It checks on launch and when returning to the foreground, normally reusing its cache if less than one hour has passed since the last successful check; failed attempts are also throttled. The request does not contain your gameplay records, favorites, or purchase history. Announcement requests disable cookies.

GitHub also hosts these legal pages through GitHub Pages. These pages contain no analytics, tracking scripts, embedded third-party content, or cookies set by us. GitHub receives ordinary request information and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Opening an external link is subject to the destination’s privacy practices.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the app, preserve your choices and records, account for the free gameplay trial, provide and restore purchased access, deliver announcements, prevent misuse, and answer support or legal requests. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to send. Do not send passwords or full payment card details.

Pocket Play has no advertising SDK or separate gameplay analytics SDK and does not use information for cross-app advertising tracking. Purchase reporting provided by RevenueCat is distinct from gameplay tracking. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing needed to provide requested features or purchases supports performance of our agreement with you; security and support support legitimate interests, subject to your rights; legally required retention supports compliance with legal obligations.

Service providers and international processing

Apple provides the App Store, payment and subscription systems. RevenueCat processes purchase-related data on our behalf to support Premium access. GitHub provides announcement and website hosting. These providers may process information outside your country; their policies and applicable contractual safeguards govern their handling of information. We do not promise that data remains in a particular country.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain in the app’s storage until removed with the app or otherwise deleted through your device’s data controls. Offloading an app can retain its data. Backups may retain copies, and restoring a backup can restore local records. There is no Pocket Play cloud account or in-app account-deletion feature.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, erase Apple’s transaction records, or automatically delete RevenueCat’s server-side records. Purchase-related records are retained as needed to provide and restore access and to meet applicable legal, security, and dispute-resolution requirements. Provider retention rules may also apply.

Contact gungorb860@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of information handled for Pocket Play. We may need limited information to locate and verify a purchase-related record because the app does not collect your identity. We cannot remotely access or recover gameplay records held only on your device. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request and any applicable legal obligations.

Children’s privacy

Pocket Play does not ask for a date of birth and has no chat or public user profiles. The purchase and technical processing described above also applies when a child uses the app. Parents and guardians should supervise use and purchases and can use Apple’s parental controls. If you believe a child’s personal information has been provided or processed without legally required permission, contact gungorb860@gmail.com so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, and complain to a competent privacy authority. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing. Rights may be subject to legal exceptions and reasonable identity verification. Contact gungorb860@gmail.com to make a request. Apple controls requests relating to its account and payment records; GitHub controls its own hosting security logs.

Security and policy updates

The app’s service requests use HTTPS. No system or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top identifies the current version; material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. The current policy remains available from Settings and the Premium paywall.
